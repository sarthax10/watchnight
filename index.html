About the game

Everything you need before your first night at the manor, in plain words.

The story

Nobody comes to the manor by choice.

Everyone in the house has done something they can't take back, and for it they have been sent away from the world. They are delivered in the rain to a gatehouse at the foot of the hill, made to sign the Register of the Banished, and driven up to the house in a black carriage.

The register says why each of them was sent, but the crimes are inked out. Everyone did something. Nobody knows what the others did.

The house is where they serve their sentence, and its rules are simple: do your work, sit at the Vigil each evening, lock your door at night, and pray to lighten your sin.

But the banished are not alone in the house. Something old lives there, and it has chosen one of them. By day it looks and talks like them. By night it hunts.

What it is

Watchnight is a horror game about trust, for one to eight players.

A group of sinners has been banished to an old manor house to repent. One of them is secretly cursed: one of you by day, a killer by night. Everyone else has to work out who it is and vote it out before it gets them.

Playing alone? The house fills the empty places with its own residents, who do their jobs, talk, lie and vote just as people do.

  • One to eight players
  • Alone or online
  • Up to six days and nights

The house

An isolated manor in the hills, in the summer of 1984.

Twelve bedrooms, a great hall with one long table, a kitchen, a chapel, a library, a walled garden with an old oak at its heart, and a graveyard beyond the wall.

By day the house is warm and busy: the fires are lit and the cat sleeps by the hearth. At night the rain comes in, the candles burn down, and the portraits on the walls open their eyes.

By day: your job

Every morning you're given a job, and the house only runs if everyone does theirs.

There are eight: gardener, cook, launderer, cleaner, firekeeper, chapel keeper, steward, and the one who keeps the locks and lamps. They lean on each other. The gardener grows what the cook needs, the cook needs the firekeeper's fire, and if supper is on the table by sunset, everyone sleeps easier.

Finished work earns merit. Spend it at Abel's shop on things for the night: a candle of your own, an iron bolt for your door, a tonic to run further, or a token that tells you how heavy someone's sin is.

Your job gives you reasons to be in certain rooms. Being somewhere without one is how people get noticed.

At dusk: the Vigil

When the bell rings, everyone sits down together at the long table.

This is where you talk. Who was out of their room last night? Who skipped their work? Whose story doesn't match what you saw? Then everyone votes, and whoever gets the most votes leaves the house for good.

Choose carefully. Send away an innocent and the curse is still among you, with one fewer person to stop it.

At night: the curse hunts

After the Vigil everyone goes to their room, and the curse goes looking.

It can wear the face of anyone in the house, so the person you saw in the corridor may not have been who you think. Lock your door. If you hear it coming, hide under your bed.

When someone dies, their body has to be carried to the graveyard and buried. Every body left lying in the house makes the night worse for everyone.

Sin

Everyone carries a little sin, and it grows every night.

It grows faster while the dead lie unburied, and when nobody ate supper. If yours fills up, you die. You can bring it down by praying at the chapel altar, as long as its candles are lit.

The cursed one's sin climbs far faster than anyone's. When it fills, the curse has to kill before dawn, or it takes its own host instead.

How you win

At the start, one of the banished is told in secret that they carry the curse. It could be you.

If you're one of the banished

Find the cursed one and vote them out at the Vigil, or stay alive until its own curse takes it. Do your job, watch each other, and bury your dead.

If you're the curse

Last six nights, or thin the house out until only one other soul is left. Do your job like everyone else. Wear their faces. Lie at the table.

Secret roles

Two others are dealt a secret along with their job. Neither is ever the curse.

The Watcher
At night, can look through the eyes of the house: its portraits and its statues.
The Investigator
Holds the only investigation kit in the house, and can study one body or one person each night.

What they learn is only worth something if the others believe them.

Playing it

Watchnight is a free playtest for Windows, and it's still being made.

Play alone with the house's residents, or online with up to eight: one person hosts a manor and shares its Manor Pass, and the others join with it. Press F8 anywhere in the game to send a report straight to the developer.
