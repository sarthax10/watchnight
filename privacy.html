Privacy Policy

What the Watchnight website and game collect, why, who sees it, and how to have it removed. We collect as little as we can, sell nothing, and show no ads.

Last updated 22 October 2026.

Who we are

Watchnight is an independent game, published as a free playtest on itch.io by its developer (sarthax10). "We" in this policy means the developer. You can reach us about anything here through the Watchnight Discord or by message on the itch.io page.

The website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no trackers. The fonts, pictures and sounds are served from this site itself.
  • The guest book count. The first time your browser opens the site, it asks our server (a Cloudflare Worker) to add one to the visitor count, and remembers on your device that it has signed (and its line number) so it is counted only once. To stop scripts inflating the count, the server keeps a one-way hash of your IP address mixed with the date, only for that day; it is deleted the next day and cannot be turned back into your address. Nothing else about you is stored.
  • Stored on your device (browser storage, never sent to us): your choices on this page (lights, sound), your record in the "guests" game, whether you have seen the opening, the guest book line, and an offline copy of the site's files (a service worker) so it loads fast. Clear your browser's site data to remove all of it.
  • Third parties your browser talks to: GitHub Pages hosts the site (GitHub keeps standard server logs: see GitHub's privacy statement); the Discord member count is read from Discord's public invite API (Discord's privacy policy); our counter runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare's privacy policy). Links to YouTube, Instagram and itch.io take you to those sites, under their own policies.

The game

  • Playing alone sends nothing anywhere. The game keeps a short log of the session on your own computer (in its data folder) to help with bug reports.
  • Feedback (F8). Only when you press Send, your report goes to the developer's private Discord channel: the type of report, what you wrote, the name you chose to give (optional), a screenshot of the game, a map of where you stood, any files you attached, and the session log (the game's version, your room and position, the time of day, your operating system and graphics card, what you did in the session, and any errors). We use it only to fix and improve the game, and delete it when it is no longer needed. If sending fails, the report is saved on your computer instead.
  • Playing online. To connect players, the game uses our pairing server (on Cloudflare) with a six-digit Manor Pass; the server passes connection messages between players and keeps nothing once the match starts. Players then connect directly to each other (WebRTC), so, as in any online game or voice call, the players' computers learn each other's IP addresses (the game never shows them). The host's computer runs the match and receives each player's moves, chat and voice to pass them on; nothing is recorded. Your player name is shown to the other players.
  • Voice chat is off until you hold the talk key (or turn on open mic in Settings). It goes only to the players near you in the game, through the host, and is never recorded or stored.
  • Discord. If the Discord app is running, the game shows "Playing Watchnight" and where you are in a match (never anything secret) on your Discord profile, through the Discord app on your own computer. You can turn this off with "Show on Discord" in the pause menu or in Settings, under Privacy.
  • Updates. The game checks the public itch.io page for a newer version, and downloads it from itch.io when you choose to update. itch.io sees those requests like any web visit (itch.io's privacy policy).

Why we use it (legal bases)

The visitor count and the anti-abuse hash: our legitimate interest in showing an honest count and keeping it free of spam. Feedback reports: your consent, given when you press Send. Online play, voice and chat: to provide the game you asked to play. We never sell or share data for advertising, and never use it to build profiles.

How long we keep it

The visitor count is a single number. The daily hashes are deleted every day. Feedback reports are kept only as long as they are useful for fixing the game (at most two years), then deleted. Nothing from online play is kept.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask to see, correct or delete anything we hold about you (in practice, a feedback report you sent), or object to its use, and we will do it. Tell us which report it was (roughly when you sent it, and the name you gave) through the Discord or itch.io. If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK you may also complain to your data protection authority; in India, to the Data Protection Board; in California you have the rights given by the CCPA (we do not sell or share personal information).

Children

Watchnight is a horror game with frightening scenes and violence, made for players aged 13 and over (we suggest 16 and over). It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. If you believe a child has sent us a report, tell us and we will delete it.

Security

Reports go over encrypted connections to a private channel only the developer can read. The pairing server and the counter keep no personal data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes, and important changes are announced on the Discord and in the game's patch notes.
